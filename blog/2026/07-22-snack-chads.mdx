### Chads.. with snacks?

Yep! Well.. The character isn't a chad judging by art, but definitely a chad in spirit! The game's got four levels that you can play through, speed run, enjoy slowly. Perhaps you can spend time trying to break the game.

**I'm sure that last one will happen.**

> I fixed all the bugs!
>
> — Me before finding a bug

Today marks game #6 complete after a long time, and with a long break in-between. However, I'm proud of myself to have pushed through despite that, and go all the way to complete and release it.

As always, this game definitely is the best one I made so far, perhaps not that much in visible gameplay, but technically it's a lot more work than all previous games have been.

#### A part of real-life

A small one, but an important one. I like to take _walks_, whether it's just for physical activity, or with some specific goal in mind. I do a "snack walk" almost every single day, which is a decently long walk that always results in the purchase of a small snack.

This quickly inspired me to theme the mario clone after these walks. Oh, and that immediately gave me the idea for the game's name.

"Snack walk" + "Mario Bros" => "Snack Chads".

A development post will become available at a later time, where we'll talk about the advanced player controller, and composition. Please look forward to it!

### Let's play!

The game's available on itch.io, so [give it a try](https://problem-18.itch.io/snack-chads) and let me know what you think! I'm certain you'll have a nice time!

Thanks for playing! :heart:
